### Reading notes

- GAIT Recognition is remote biometric indentification Technology.
- can achieve identification at the long distance.
- Varied application : Criminal investigation, medical treatment, identity recognition, Human Computer Interaction(HCI).
- State of the art(SOTA) GAIT recognition techniques : 3D and 2D based methods.
- GAIT Dataset : Human walking model, Static & Dynamic feature extractions.
- View Invariant.
- Person's walking style : Unique behavioural characteristics.
- Non-contactable and non-invasive.
- Usually, GAIT Recogniton includes following five steps :
- Aquisition of GAIT Data
- Preprosessing
- Period Extractions
- Feature Extractions
- Classification
- How to recognize the GAIT ? : The GAIT is aquired by single camera, multiple camera and professional motion capture system, camera with depth sensor.
